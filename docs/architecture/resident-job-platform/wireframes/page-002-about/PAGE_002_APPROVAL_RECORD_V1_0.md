## Approval Basis

The approval is based on the accepted controlled Revision 1 and the Product Design Review Revision 1.

Revision 1 resolved the documented condition:

- Strengthen the "Why Trust Resident Job" section.

The approved wireframe now communicates trust through mechanisms:

- Verified worker onboarding process.
- Credential verification workflow.
- Background check process where available and applicable.
- Attendance verification.
- Secure document handling.
- AI-assisted workforce matching with human oversight.
- Operational transparency.
- Quality assurance process.
- Privacy and security commitments.
- Auditability.
- Clear communication between employers and workers.

## Guardrails

This approval does not approve unsupported trust claims.

The approved wireframe does not include:

- Fake testimonials.
- Fake statistics.
- Invented customer names.
- Unsupported certifications.
- Country-specific regulatory claims.
- Claims that the platform already operates at scale.
