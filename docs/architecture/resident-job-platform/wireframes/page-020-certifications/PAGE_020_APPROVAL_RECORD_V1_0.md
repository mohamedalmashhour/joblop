## 2. Approved Scope

Page 020 defines the authenticated worker Certifications experience.

Approved capabilities at wireframe level:

- View certification records.
- Add certification information.
- Classify source authority.
- Link supporting evidence through the Documents workflow.
- Track review, validity, requirement, and source states independently.
- Understand required/recommended/optional/employer-specific certification context.
- Respond to correction, rejection, expiry, and verification-unavailable states.
- Preserve privacy and employer visibility boundaries.

## 3. Approval Basis

The approved revision satisfies the documented owner conditions:

- Licence and certification concepts remain separate.
- Source authority is clear.
- Review, validity, requirement, record, and source states are independent.
- Expired records cannot appear operationally valid.
- Internal training is not presented as regulator-equivalent.
- Required certifications have an explainable approved source.
- Employer access is restricted to relevant operational facts.
- Upload cannot be mistaken for verification.
- Certification cannot be mistaken for assignment approval.
- AI cannot verify, approve, create, reject, or rank qualifications.
- Worker Compliance Relationship Map is consistent.
