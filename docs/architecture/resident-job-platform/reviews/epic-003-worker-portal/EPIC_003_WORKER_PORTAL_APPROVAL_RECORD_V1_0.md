## Accepted Planning Principles

Worker Portal is a personal operational workspace.

Worker Dashboard is not:

- Scheduler Console.
- Employer Portal.
- Administration.
- Payroll.

Worker Portal must expose only worker-appropriate functionality and protect worker profile, documents, earnings, messages, attendance, location, calendar, and AI recommendation data.
