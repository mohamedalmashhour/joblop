## Approval Basis

The approval is based on controlled Revision 1 and the Product Design Review Revision 1.

Revision 1 resolved the documented condition:

- Reorganize the shared operating flow into before-shift, during-shift, and after-shift phases.

The approved wireframe now separates:

- Worker responsibilities.
- Employer responsibilities.
- Platform responsibilities.
- AI recommendations.
- Human review.
- Final decisions.
