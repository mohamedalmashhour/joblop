## 2. Approved Scope

Page 022 defines the authenticated worker Availability Calendar experience.

Approved capabilities at wireframe level:

- Define recurring worker availability.
- Add one-time availability.
- Mark unavailable periods.
- Manage date-specific exceptions.
- Separate availability from preferences.
- Review accepted assignments and protected commitments.
- Detect and explain conflicts.
- Clarify timezone, travel, daylight-saving, and overnight contexts.
- Display staffing readiness indicators without creating dispatch decisions.
- Preserve worker privacy and employer visibility boundaries.

## 3. Approval Basis

The approved revision satisfies the documented owner conditions:

- Availability, preference, assignment, and commitment remain independent.
- Recurring scheduling is unambiguous.
- Assignment protection is enforced conceptually.
- Timezone behavior is explicit.
- Conflict resolution is safe.
- Employer visibility respects worker privacy.
- AI cannot alter commitments.
- No governance or operational risk remains at wireframe level.
