## 1. Executive Summary

Revision 1 resolves the owner conditions for Page 053 - Ratings. The revised wireframe makes
rating eligibility governed, rating lifecycle states truthful, mutual rating visibility fair,
retaliation protections explicit, moderation and appeals bounded, aggregate ratings contextual,
hidden reputation scoring prohibited, privacy protected, permissions role-specific, auditability
complete, and AI strictly advisory.

The page is ready to freeze as Approved Wireframe v1.0. Page 054 - Messages is ready to become the
next controlled gate, but no Page 054 files are created.

| Owner condition | Revision 1 result |
