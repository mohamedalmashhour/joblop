## 1. Approved Scope

Page 016 is approved as the worker's operational home screen.

Approved purpose:

- Show the worker what they need to do now.
- Show whether the worker is currently on shift.
- Surface immediate operational alerts.
- Show the next scheduled shift.
- Identify anything blocking work.
- Provide secondary access to calendar, availability, upcoming shifts, and recommended
  opportunities.
- Provide tertiary access to earnings summary, messages, notifications, documents, and profile
  completion.

## 3. Approval Basis

Page 016 is approved because Revision 1 resolved the documented conditions:

- Operational priority is clear.
- Widget hierarchy is Primary / Secondary / Tertiary.
- Recommended, assigned, accepted, and active shift states are separated.
- Earnings are informational and not payroll.
- AI suggestions are separate from operational status.
- Mobile first-screen density is controlled.
- The page remains worker-focused.
- No scheduler, employer, admin, or payroll functionality appears.
