## 12. Security Summary

Security requirements include least privilege, scoped search, guarded actions, reason capture, confirmation, restricted exports, audit integrity, stale-data warnings, and truthful access-denied states.

## 13. Privacy Summary

The architecture excludes passwords, secrets, tokens, recovery codes, full identity documents, banking data, tax data, health data, unrelated complaints, investigation material, fraud signals, private messages, precise off-duty location, and protected personal information outside authorized scope.

## 14. AI Governance Summary

AI remains advisory only. It may summarize visible records, explain policy, identify missing information, identify inconsistencies, draft human-reviewed notes, suggest navigation, and compare governed options. It cannot write authoritative state or perform protected actions.

## 15. Accessibility Summary

Every page includes semantic headings, keyboard navigation, screen-reader states, accessible tables and forms, non-color-only statuses, focus management, readable timestamps, plain-language errors, large mobile touch targets, and accessible empty/stale states.

## 16. International Readiness Summary
