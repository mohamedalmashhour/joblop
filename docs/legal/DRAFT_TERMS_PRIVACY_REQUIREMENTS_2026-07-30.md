# DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED

## Terms and Privacy Requirements for Legal Review

These are product requirements, not legal terms, legal advice or permission to enable
registration. Employer self-registration remains fail-closed until Legal approves immutable
`TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_NOTICE_VERSION` references and the acceptance evidence contract.

### Terms requirements

- Identify the marketplace operator, contracting parties, service scope and legal invoice issuer.
- Define employer and worker eligibility, independent-work relationship, prohibited use and account
  responsibility without letting product code determine legal classification.
- Define staffing request, offer, assignment, cancellation, attendance, timesheet, dispute,
  invoice, marketplace-fee, collection, refund and credit-note rules.
- State payment authorization/capture timing, failed collection handling and that card choice does
  not create a Québec card-specific surcharge.
- Define governing law, dispute route, suspension/termination, limitation provisions and document
  precedence.
- Provide English/French requirements and identify which language/version governs.

### Privacy requirements

- Identify controller/organization roles and purposes for employer, worker, admin, security,
  payment, audit and support data.
- Define collection, consent/authority, minimization, access, correction, portability, deletion,
  retention, legal hold, backups, subprocessors and cross-border transfer requirements.
- Cover authentication metadata, device/session data, approximate job locations, documents,
  communications, invoice/payment references and audit records.
- Prohibit raw card/bank credential storage and distinguish provider tokens from payment data.
- Define breach, complaint and privacy-contact procedures plus Québec-specific review items.
- Confirm whether and how synthetic staging evidence may be retained.

### Acceptance evidence contract

Legal approval must produce an immutable version identifier, content checksum, effective date,
locale, notice/consent rule, supersession rule and approved public URL. Registration must record
the accepted versions, actor, timestamp and evidence without storing passwords or reset tokens.
Material changes require a versioned re-acceptance decision.

### Explicit non-activation

Draft content, repository presence, owner review, or a configured URL alone is insufficient.
Registration remains disabled until the approved versions and D7 evidence are supplied and staging
tests prove the exact content, checkbox, keyboard flow, audit record and denial when acceptance is
absent.
