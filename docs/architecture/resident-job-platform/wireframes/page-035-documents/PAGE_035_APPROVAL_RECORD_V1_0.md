## 2. Approved Scope

Page 035 defines the authenticated worker Documents experience.

Approved capabilities at wireframe level:

- View authorized document metadata, status, version, expiry, access, verification, and retention
  information.
- Understand document owner, uploader, issuer, reviewer, verifier, requester, and viewer roles.
- Distinguish upload, review, verification, validity, version, access, retention, and hold states.
- Access governed upload, replacement, view, download, support, and correction entry points where
  authorized.
- Preserve version chain, replacement relationship, historical versions, duplicate detection, and
  correction workflow.
- Protect sensitive identity, licence, certification, payroll, tax, banking, background-check,
  contract, legal-evidence, and dispute-evidence documents.
- Preserve conceptual auditability for upload, replace, verify, download, view, hold, release, and
  delete actions.
- Receive AI assistance only for status explanation, expiry explanation, visible metadata summary,
  and next-action guidance.

## 3. Approval Basis

The approved revision satisfies all documented conditions:

- Ownership is governed.
- Lifecycle remains truthful.
- Versioning is preserved.
- Employer access is minimal.
- Retention and holds are documented.
- Privacy is preserved.
- Auditability is complete.
- AI cannot approve, verify, alter, delete, or disclose documents.
- No governance, compliance, privacy, or operational risks remain at wireframe level.

## 4. Explicit Boundaries

Documents does not:

- Own every underlying document workflow.
- Treat uploaded as owned.
- Treat owned as verified.
- Treat verified as globally accepted.
- Treat current as verified.
- Treat superseded as deleted.
- Treat replacement as historical loss.
- Permit broad employer access to worker documents.
- Expose tax, banking, identity images, dispute evidence, legal-hold information, or unrelated
  documents to employers.
- Expose ID numbers, tax identifiers, banking information, background report contents, protected
  health information, or legal evidence in previews.
- Allow deletion eligibility to mean deletion.
- Allow holds to mean permanent retention.
- Own object storage, OCR, antivirus, document scanning, identity verification, background provider,
  licence verification, retention engine, legal-hold engine, e-signature, or fraud engine.
- Allow AI to approve, reject, verify, sign, delete, alter, release holds, expose restricted
  documents, or determine eligibility.
