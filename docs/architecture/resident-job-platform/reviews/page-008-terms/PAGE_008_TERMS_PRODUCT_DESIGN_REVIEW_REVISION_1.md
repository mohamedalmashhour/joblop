## Acknowledgement Clarity

Passed.

The wireframe clearly distinguishes:

- Public Viewing: anyone may read; no acknowledgement required.
- Account Registration: applicable Terms shown; acknowledgement requested before account completion
  where configured.
- Existing Users: current-Terms state continues until future operational/legal rules require
  notification.
- Updated Terms: new version may require review and acknowledgement based on future
  operational/legal configuration.
