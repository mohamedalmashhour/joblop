## 2. Approved Scope

Page 032 defines the authenticated worker Ratings experience.

Approved capabilities at wireframe level:

- View governed ratings and feedback connected to completed shifts.
- Understand rating eligibility and reviewer authority.
- Distinguish worker ratings, employer/residence ratings, private operational feedback, public
  reputation, and internal quality signals.
- Understand individual ratings, category scores, rolling averages, lifetime reputation, public
  reputation, moderation status, and matching signals as independent concepts.
- View submission, publication, moderation, appeal, removal, restoration, correction, and dispute
  states.
- Respond through governed response paths where permitted.
- Report retaliation and fairness concerns.
- Understand public/private visibility boundaries.
- Preserve audit history for rating lifecycle events.
- Receive AI assistance only for explanation, respectful drafting, prohibited-content detection, and
  support routing.

## 3. Approval Basis

The approved revision satisfies all documented conditions:

- Reviewer authority is governed.
- Rating states remain independent.
- Retaliation protections are clear.
- Moderation lifecycle is complete.
- Privacy boundaries are preserved.
- Auditability is documented.
- AI cannot influence reputation decisions.
- No governance, fairness, or privacy risks remain at wireframe level.

## 4. Explicit Boundaries

Ratings does not:

- Fabricate ratings.
- Create a universal worker score.
- Automatically rank workers.
- Automatically penalize workers.
- Prove professional competence.
- Determine worker eligibility.
- Replace licences, certifications, background checks, or availability.
- Create hidden blacklists.
- Decide misconduct.
- Publish private feedback automatically.
- Silently remove or alter feedback.
- Expose resident information.
- Expose protected identities unnecessarily.
- Expose unrelated worker data.
- Own messaging.
- Own notifications.
- Own formal dispute workflow.
- Own fraud detection.
- Own reputation engine.
- Own recommendation engine.
- Own search ranking.
- Own employer matching.
- Allow AI to publish, remove, calculate reputation, override moderation, expose protected
  identities, decide misconduct, or determine eligibility.
