## Mission

Resident Job is an operational workforce platform for senior residences, healthcare facilities,
retirement homes, clinics, and long-term care organizations.

It is not a generic job board. It is designed to help operators fill urgent staffing shortages,
verify workers, schedule shifts, track attendance, bill clients, pay workers, and use AI-assisted
operations with human approval.

## Architecture Rule

No production implementation begins until page architecture, UX, database/API requirements,
permissions, validation, and AI behavior are approved page by page.

2. Users
3. UI Layout
4. Components

6. Data Model
7. APIs
8. Permissions
9. Validation
10. AI Features
11. Mobile Design
12. Desktop Design
13. Future Expansion

## AI Requirements

Every page must define whether the AI assistant may provide:

- Suggested actions
- Risk detection
- Missing information detection
- Recommendations
- Fraud detection
- Compliance checks
- Productivity scoring
- Automation opportunities

AI remains advisory unless a future approved architecture explicitly authorizes a narrow
automation. Any action that affects workers, clients, money, compliance, scheduling, or external
communications requires recorded human approval.
