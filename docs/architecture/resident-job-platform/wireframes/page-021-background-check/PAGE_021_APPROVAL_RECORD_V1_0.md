## 2. Approved Scope

Page 021 defines the authenticated worker Background Check experience.

Approved capabilities at wireframe level:

- View why a background screening is requested.
- Understand role, employer, assignment, jurisdiction, or policy requirement context.
- Give explicit informed consent.
- Decline or ask questions.
- Understand third-party provider boundary.
- Track requirement, consent, screening process, and platform review states independently.
- View safe non-sensitive status information.
- Use correction, dispute, provider support, and platform support paths.
- Understand expiry or renewal where applicable.
- Understand employer visibility boundaries.

## 3. Approval Basis

The approved revision satisfies the documented owner conditions:

- Requirement, consent, process, and review remain independent.
- Worker rights are clearly explained.
- Provider authority is separate from platform authority.
- Employer visibility is appropriately restricted.
- Identity and background screening remain separate.
- Expiry is handled correctly.
- AI cannot evaluate or approve workers.
- Neutral operational language is used throughout.
- No governance or privacy risk remains at wireframe level.
