# Legal Content Governance Standard v1.0

Status: Active governance standard
Project: Resident Job
Applies to: Terms of Use, Privacy, Cookie Notice, Accessibility Statement, AI Transparency Notice,
Data Processing Addendum, Compliance Notices, and future jurisdiction-specific legal documents
Implementation: Not authorized
Legal publication: Not authorized

## Purpose

This standard provides one reusable governance model for all legal-facing documents in Resident Job.

It defines how legal-facing content is planned, reviewed, versioned, approved, published, superseded,
and archived without allowing product documentation to become final legal wording.

## 1. Product Team Responsibilities

The Product Team may define:

- Page structure.
- Navigation.
- Reading experience.
- Acknowledgement UX.
- Version display.
- Change-history placement.
- Help and escalation paths.
- Governance metadata.
- User journey and accessibility requirements.
- AI transparency patterns.

The Product Team must not create final legal clauses or publish legal-facing content without the
required review gates.

## 2. Operations Responsibilities

Operations may review:

- Operational feasibility.
- Support routing.
- User acknowledgement workflow.
- Customer support impact.
- Worker and employer operational implications.
- Internal escalation paths.
- Change communication needs.

Operations must not finalize legal obligations, legal remedies, jurisdictional rights, or contract
language.

## 3. Legal Responsibilities

Legal review is responsible for:

- Final legal wording.
- Contractual obligations.
- Jurisdiction-specific requirements.
- Governing-law language.
- Dispute-resolution language.
- Liability, warranty, indemnity, and limitation language.
- Regulatory references.
- Statutory rights.
- Legal deadlines.
- Legal interpretations.
- Final publication approval for legal-facing content.

## 4. Publication Workflow

Legal-facing content follows this lifecycle:

```text
Draft
→ Product Approved
→ Operations Reviewed
→ Legal Approved
→ Jurisdiction Approved
→ Published
→ Superseded
→ Archived
```

No legal-facing content may be considered publishable until it reaches Legal Approved and, where
applicable, Jurisdiction Approved.

## 5. Version Control

Every legal-facing document must track:

- Document ID.
- Version.
- Status.
- Content owner.
- Legal owner placeholder.
- Operational reviewer placeholder.
- Jurisdiction scope.
- Effective-date placeholder.
- Superseded-by placeholder.
- Archive reference placeholder.

Do not fabricate dates, version history, reviewer names, legal owners, or jurisdiction approvals.

## 6. Jurisdiction Handling

Legal-facing documents must remain jurisdiction-neutral unless localization and legal review
authorize jurisdiction-specific language.

Country, province, state, regulator, statutory right, deadline, tax, payroll, healthcare, or
employment references require legal and localization review before publication.

## 7. Change Approval Process

Changes to legal-facing content must identify:

- Trigger.
- Requested change.
- Affected documents.
- Affected user groups.
- Operational impact.
- Legal review need.
- Jurisdiction impact.
- User acknowledgement need.
- Publication plan.

No silent legal-content changes are allowed.

## 8. Legal Review Gate

The Legal Review Gate confirms:

- Final legal wording is reviewed.
- Required jurisdictions are reviewed.
- Version and effective-date fields are controlled.
- Acknowledgement requirements are defined.
- Superseded document handling is defined.
- Publication is authorized.

## 9. Emergency Update Process

Emergency legal updates must:

- Identify the risk.
- Identify affected documents.
- Mark status as emergency review.
- Route to legal and operations.
- Record the decision.
- Preserve the previous version.
- Publish only after approval.
- Archive or supersede the prior version.

Do not publish emergency legal changes through product design alone.

## 10. Retirement / Archive Process

When legal-facing content is retired:

- Mark the document as Superseded or Archived.
- Identify replacement content where applicable.
- Preserve the prior version.
- Preserve approval history.
- Preserve user acknowledgement history where applicable.
- Remove active navigation only after approval.

## AI Rules

AI may:

- Summarize approved legal-facing content.
- Flag inconsistencies.
- Suggest missing sections for human review.
- Identify documents that may need review.
- Assist with change-impact checklists.

AI must not:

- Draft final contractual language for publication.
- Invent limitation-of-liability wording.
- Invent governing-law clauses.
- Invent dispute-resolution procedures.
- Interpret statutory rights.
- Publish legal content.
- Approve legal changes.
- Remove required metadata.

## Implementation Boundary

This document defines governance only.

It does not authorize:

- Legal publication.
- Frontend implementation.
- Backend implementation.
- Document management implementation.
- Consent or acknowledgement storage.
- Production data.
- Deployment.
