## Change Control

Allowed changes:

- Factual corrections
- Broken link/path corrections
- Approved architecture change requests

Forbidden changes without approval:

- Adding or removing pages
- Changing role authority
- Weakening AI advisory limits
- Mixing client billing, worker pay, and platform margin
- Removing human approval gates
- Beginning frontend implementation from this freeze
